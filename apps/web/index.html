Which key do I use?

Use the 12-digit setup key during Wi-Fi setup. On your home network, use the 32-character owner key from USB pairing.

Join Wi-Fi

Enter a 2.4 GHz network. You can use automatic time or enter a preferred NTP server.

Open DISPATCH on your home network

  1. Reconnect this phone or computer to the Wi-Fi network you selected.
  2. Find the controller’s new IP address in your router’s connected-device list or on the USB console.
  3. Open that address in your browser and enter your permanent owner key.

Bookmark the dashboard after opening it. A DHCP reservation keeps the address stable.
