## 1. Parties, contributions, and ownership

This agreement is between SPECTRE WORKS LLC (Company) and the individual or
entity explicitly accepting it (Contributor). A Contribution is material
Contributor deliberately submits for incorporation into DISPATCH, identified
by pull request, commit, patch, or another recorded submission. Material
marked “not a contribution,” private support data, and unrelated work are
excluded. Identify third-party material separately.

Contributor retains ownership of its original Contributions. This agreement
is a nonexclusive rights grant, not an assignment, and does not stop Contributor
using or licensing its own original work elsewhere. Contributor's reuse of
Company or third-party material still requires the relevant permission.

## 2. Copyright permission to Company

To the extent Contributor controls the rights, Contributor grants Company a
perpetual, worldwide, nonexclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license
to reproduce, use, prepare and distribute modifications, publicly display and
perform, and distribute Contributions in source or object form, and to
sublicense those rights through multiple tiers.

This expressly includes distribution under DISPATCH's source-available terms,
permissive or copyleft terms selected for particular components, and proprietary
commercial agreements, with or without charge. No contributor royalty or
share of commercial revenue is promised. Company is not required to accept,
release, or maintain any Contribution.

This CLA grants rights to Company; it is not by itself a permissive public
license to all DISPATCH code. Recipients receive the license Company actually
applies to a release, subject to rights Company can grant.

## 3. Patent permission

Contributor grants Company a worldwide, nonexclusive, royalty-free patent
license, with the sublicensing rights needed to distribute and authorize use
of Contributions under the selected product terms, to make, have made, use,
import, offer, and sell the Contributions and their incorporated combinations
with DISPATCH. It covers only claims Contributor controls that are necessarily
infringed by the Contribution alone or its combination with the project to
which it is submitted; it is not a license to unrelated patent claims.

For an entity that brings patent litigation alleging that the Contribution or
its submitted project combination infringes a patent, the patent permissions
under this clause to that entity terminate to the extent legally permitted.
Counsel should verify the scope of sublicensing and defensive termination
before adoption; no broader patent warranty is implied.

## 4. Authority and provenance

Contributor confirms authority to grant these rights. If an employer or other
entity owns relevant rights, obtain and record its authorization or a suitable
entity agreement before acceptance. An employee cannot license an employer's
rights merely by signing in a personal capacity.

Contributor identifies known third-party material, licenses, restrictions,
and relevant provenance included in a submission. Do not relabel another
project's code or claim original authorship without basis. Disclose AI-assisted
or generated material where rights, provenance, or applicable project policy
require it; Company must not infer protectable ownership from a header alone.

Contributor will promptly correct material information it learns is inaccurate.
This is not a promise to conduct a patent search or guarantee that no unknown
third-party right exists.

## 5. Contributions as-is

Except for the authority and provenance confirmations above and mandatory
law, Contributions are provided AS IS without implied warranties. Contributor
has no continuing support, update, or indemnity obligation unless separately
agreed. No employment, partnership, or compensation arrangement is created.

## 6. Scope of acceptance and private records

Acceptance must identify the Contributor, authority/capacity, agreement
version, date, and whether it applies to specified submissions or expressly
identified future contributions. Company records evidence privately using
only information reasonably needed to establish rights.

A Git author name, sign-off, or PR checkbox alone is not claimed to be an
executed CLA. Do not publish residence addresses, signatures, or contact data.
Public attribution may use the Contributor's chosen public identity.

## 7. Other rights and amendments

No trademark right or right to imply endorsement is granted. Third-party
licenses remain intact. This agreement cannot create rights the Contributor
does not possess. Existing recipients' valid grants are not withdrawn merely
because Contributor stops contributing.

Changes to this agreement require express acceptance. Governing-law,
acceptance, moral-rights, and entity-signature provisions must be finalized
for the intended contributor population before adoption. An agent must not
invent evidence that a contributor agreed.
