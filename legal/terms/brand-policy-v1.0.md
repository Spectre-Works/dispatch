# SPECTRE WORKS / DISPATCH Brand Policy v1.0

Effective 2026-11-23 for brand rights held by SPECTRE WORKS LLC.

## 1. Scope

SPECTRE WORKS, DISPATCH, DISPATCH/01, official logos/wordmarks, release badges,
and separately identified brand artwork communicate product origin and
support. Rights in those materials are reserved except for the limited uses
expressly allowed here, another written grant, or applicable law.

Application or documentation licensing does not automatically license logos.
An Apache integration does not imply endorsement by SPECTRE WORKS.

## 2. Truthful references

Subject to applicable law, plain-text references may truthfully identify
compatibility, an actual integration, the origin of code, or a genuine product.
Use wording such as “compatible with DISPATCH” where accurate, rather than
“official DISPATCH product” for an independent offering. Do not imply a
partnership, certification, warranty, or support relationship that does not
exist. This policy does not restrict lawful nominative or other fair use.

## 3. Unmodified official software and genuine hardware

Where the software license independently permits redistribution of an
unmodified official release, the rights holder permits retaining its original
in-application branding and accompanying original identification materials
as necessary to identify that authentic release. This is not permission to
manufacture new branded hardware or alter notices to suggest endorsement.

A reseller may truthfully identify a genuine used DISPATCH/01 device and use
its own photographs of that device to offer it for sale. Disclose material
modifications and the actual provider of support. No new or extended
SPECTRE WORKS warranty is created by resale.

## 4. Modified builds and derivatives

Modified application distributions should use a distinct primary product name
and visual identity. They may state that they derive from or interoperate
with DISPATCH, but must not use an official logo/badge or closely imitated
presentation to suggest that they are an official build.

Preserve required copyright, license, and attribution notices. Renaming a fork
is not permission to strip legal attribution. Compatibility strings needed
for interoperability can remain where they do not mislead users about origin.

This identity rule does not authorize distribution: the underlying software
license must separately permit the activity. Likewise, trademark protection
alone is not a prohibition on a properly renamed independent product.

## 5. Documentation and screenshots

Documentation expressly released under CC BY 4.0 may be adapted according to
that license. Separately identified logos and artwork are not relicensed
merely by appearing nearby. Accurate review or instructional screenshots may
show necessary incidental official branding if they do not imply endorsement,
subject to rights the publisher controls and applicable law. Do not extract
an incidental logo and reuse it as a new product identity.

## 6. Commercial co-branding and approval

New hardware branding, official certification badges, co-marketing, and
private-label arrangements require a written grant covering the actual use.
A software commercial agreement does not imply these rights unless expressly
included. Do not claim that all forks need permission merely to name the
upstream project accurately.

Before publication, provide a verified contact/approval route and complete
appropriate name-clearance review. No unverified contact address or trademark
registration number is supplied by this policy.

No part of this policy changes mandatory rights or licenses over material the
policy author does not own. Rights already granted for lawful existing uses
must be considered when adopting changes.
